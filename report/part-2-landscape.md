# PART 2 — PLATFORM LANDSCAPE: WHAT THE DATA SAYS

## 2.1 Facebook: the level playing field of formats

**The headline finding is counter-intuitive: on Facebook, the choice between image, video, text and link is close to noise.** Buffer/FrameOS analysed 45M+ Facebook posts (Mar–Aug 2026):

| Facebook format | Median engagement (45M+ posts, Mar–Aug 2026) |
|---|---|
| Images | **5.20%** |
| Video | 4.84% |
| Text-only | 4.76% |
| Links | 4.43% |

Source: Buffer/FrameOS, "Video, Carousel, or Image? What 45M Posts Say" (2026). On LinkedIn and Instagram the spread between formats is wide; on Facebook it is not. **Implication: structure, photo quality, headline clarity and posting discipline determine the winner — not the container.** A great single image beats a mediocre video; a clear text note beats a lazy link drop.

**Within that flat field, photos are still the single best bet at the top of the distribution.** NewsWhip's breakdown of Fox News's top 1,000 Facebook posts (Q2 2025, published 23 Jul 2025):

| Fox News top-1,000 posts, Q2 2025 | Posts in top 1,000 | Average engagement |
|---|---|---|
| **Photo** | 304 | **~140,000** (highest) |
| Native video | 460 | ~108,000 |
| Live video | 109 | ~117,000 |
| Link | 99 | ~66,000 (lowest) |

Additional Fox/NewsWhip findings that shaped the Top 35:

- **16 of the top 20 posts overall were photo posts.**
- Captions most frequently ran **under 40 words**.
- Videos **under 30 seconds** had a median of **82,396 interactions**.
- The labels "LIVE" and "BREAKING" were used **only when they add genuine urgency** — over-use is punished by the audience and the algorithm alike.

**Link posts work at scale — for outlets that are the destination.** The Guardian (link-post dominant) drove 15,859,000 engagements from 13,569 articles in one month (NewsWhip, Mar 2016) — but note the internal skew: its opinion section *Comment is Free* (2,785,162 engagements) beat *World News* (2,131,840). **Opinion and discussion travel further than straight news on Facebook** — a warning against a breaking-news-only feed, and the evidence base for question-led formats (F15). For a small account, a link post is the lowest-effort, lowest-engagement format (F12) — a legitimate backup, never the daily default.

**Templates beat improvisation.** BBC News used a **reusable template image** to carry story context on Facebook ("They have a template that they can use for other stories" — NewsWhip, 2016), operated by a social team of ~6 people plus an assistant editor. The template idea is the closest big-Publisher precedent for a one-person Canva workflow — formalised in this report as **F21** and **F33**.

**Branded cards scale.** Al Jazeera English's own reports: 2020 — 449M+ views, 46M engagements, 20.4M referrals to aljazeera.com; 2021 — 355M video views, 44.5M engagements, 15.7M followers. Their format mix: original social video, **branded social cards**, Facebook Stories and curated single-image posts.

**Wire-style restraint is the honesty baseline.** Reuters and AP run the shortest captions, clean un-tessellated photos, no hashtags, no exclamation marks — "strictly factual, agency-style". Dawn (Pakistan) applies the same discipline in-house: **no exclamation marks in hard news**, exact titles ("COAS"), factual captions. Adopt this voice for hard news even when the design is louder.

**Facebook image sizes that kept recurring across 2026 guides:**

| Use | Size | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Link preview (pulled automatically) | 1200 × 630 px | 1.91:1 |
| Square feed image | 1080 × 1080 px | 1:1 ("most versatile", no crop on mobile) |
| Portrait feed image | 1080 × 1350 px | 4:5 (tallest Facebook shows uncropped) |
| Stories / Reels | 1080 × 1920 px | 9:16 |

Export rules: PNG for graphics with text/logo, JPG for photos, sRGB colour, and keep headline text **out of the lower third** (UI overlap zone).

## 2.2 Instagram: carousels, curated grids and short video

**The Washington Post is the most directly useful case study in the whole corpus.** Adweek (Jul 2022): WaPo passed 6M followers, **+1.2M in 12 months**, by "making use of the gallery function to tell a story over multiple posts, relying on images and text on a color background, with more details of the story in the comments so readers have no need to leave Instagram." A team of **8–9 people** posted **~10 times/day** (Link in Bio News, Oct 2022). The two transferable mechanics: (1) **slide 1 = headline, slides 2–n = one fact each**; (2) **the caption is the full story** — the post is self-contained, and the algorithm rewards the dwell time.

**Less volume, more relevance.** BBC News halved its monthly Instagram video posts (~120 → under 60) and watched engagements rise from 10K/month (Mar 2017) to 22K/month (Feb 2018) (Digiday, Mar 2018). BBC's grid is photo-led; its Stories averaged ~100,000 views with roughly half watching to the end (OMNES Media). **For a small account this is the most encouraging evidence in the corpus: the algorithm does not require volume — it requires relevance and consistency.**

**Short video retention rules (from the strongest sources):**

| Rule | Evidence |
|---|---|
| News in the **first 3 seconds** | Sky News / Digiday 2016 |
| Under 90 sec, ideally **under 30 sec** | Sky (avg watch 45 sec); Fox (<30s median 82,396 interactions) |
| **Subtitles burned in**, always | Sky, AJ+, DW, France 24 house practice |
| One story = one short native video (not a TV clip cut down) | NowThis 15-second spots; AJ+ 99% video |
| Reel-first cadence works | France 24: 17,826,244 video views from 89 posts in Aug 2026 (~200K/post, arithmetic from Nonli figures), ~3 posts/day |

**Cards and carousels are the scalable design system.** Al Jazeera English (Shorty Awards 2020/2021): "carefully curated single-image posts, carousels, videos, lives, and **branded social cards** to break down information in an easily accessible way" — Instagram growth 1.1M (Jan 2020) → 1.85M (Dec 2020, +63%) → 2.3M (end 2021), with 420M impressions in 2021. AJ+ (99% video, 9B lifetime views) proves the ceiling of the video-first route — with a dedicated team.

**Screenshots are the cheapest viral engine — and the riskiest.** The Shade Room (28–29M followers, 174K posts, screenshot-led, community comments drive reach) and Pubity (42.2M, 240B annual views group-wide) run on curation. Their mechanics are copyable (**F13**); their copyright and misinformation risk must be engineered out — credit visible, source verified, never crop the origin.

**South Asia's speed model.** Aaj Tak became the first mainstream news channel to 15M Instagram followers (Exchange4Media, 9 Apr 2025) and drove **110M Instagram actions in Aug 2025** (51% share among Hindi news competitors) on breaking cards + speed + volume — an operation backed by a TV newsroom. The **card format** is copyable; the **volume** is not. Daily Mail accounts passed 1B video views/month (dmg media, Apr 2025) — the tabloid video ceiling, again team-powered.

**Posting intensity has a real benchmark, not a myth:** The New York Times published 2,361 Instagram posts in 2023 — **196.75/month, ~6–7/day** (MDPI study of 9,467 news posts, Jan–Dec 2023). WaPo ran ~10/day with 8–9 people. BBC ran 6–8/day on Facebook historically. **The one-person translation: 2–3 posts/day, fixed slots, plus Stories.**

## 2.4 The ten platform laws (consolidated)

1. Facebook format medians are near-identical (5.20 / 4.84 / 4.76 / 4.43%) — **clarity beats container**.
2. At the top of the Facebook distribution, **photos win** (~140K avg engagement; 16 of top 20 posts).
3. **Captions under 40 words** with exactly one CTA is the dominant high-engagement pattern (Fox/NewsWhip).
4. **Video = first 3 seconds, subtitles, under 30 seconds** for the retention curve (Sky, Fox).
5. **Carousels are Instagram's save-and-recommend engine** — slide 1 headline, one fact per slide, full story in the caption (WaPo).
6. **Fewer, better posts grow accounts** (BBC: video posts halved, engagements doubled).
7. **Templates compound** — one reusable layout system beats per-post invention (BBC template images; WaPo branded colour cards).
8. **Labels are promises.** BREAKING/LIVE only when genuine; over-use destroys the trigger (Fox/NewsWhip).
9. **Credit and verification are format features** — source line on every post; screenshots never cropped; corrections published (F22–F24).
10. **Fixed slots build habit** (audience and algorithm): 09:00 / 14:00 / 19:00 PKT, with a reactive window for true breaking news.
