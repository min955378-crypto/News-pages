# TIER 1 — DAILY DRIVERS (F01–F07)

## F01 · Single-image BREAKING photo card — the best overall Facebook format

**Platform:** FB + IG · **Evidence:** A · **Build time:** ~10 min · **Best KPI:** reach & shares

**Score profile** — Reach ★★★★★ · Retention ★★★☆☆ · Clarity ★★★★★ · Engagement ★★★★★ · Feasibility ★★★★★

**Evidence base.** Fox News top 1,000 posts Q2 2025 (NewsWhip, 23 Jul 2025): photo posts ~140K average engagement — highest of all formats; 16 of the top 20 posts were photos. BBC single-striking-photo house style; Sky News breaking single-image practice.

**Why it works.** The photo stops the scroll; the navy scrim makes one headline unmissable; the under-40-word caption answers "what happened" in one breath; the source line buys trust. It is the fastest high-evidence format to produce and it works identically on both platforms.

**Structural template (1080×1080, export 1080×1350 for IG):**

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ [BREAKING][09:00 PKT]  ← red bar, top-left (8% height max)
│                              │
│        FULL-BLEED PHOTO      │
│                              │
│▓▓▓ navy gradient scrim 40% ▓▓│
│  HEADLINE — 5–8 WORDS, CAPS  │  white Montserrat ExtraBold
│  one line of context (opt.)  │  Inter Regular
│                    Source: X │  20–24 pt, bottom-right
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

**Visual rules.** Photo from free stock (Pexels/Unsplash/Wikimedia, licence checked) or Canva stock. Navy `#0B1F3A` gradient scrim over the bottom 40%. Red `#C8102E` BREAKING bar top-left. Never text over a face. No green anywhere.

**Headline archetype:** A1 Place + verb + what — *"EARTHQUAKE HITS NORTHERN PAKISTAN"* · *"FLOODS CUT LAHORE–ISLAMABAD HIGHWAY"* · *"TALKS COLLAPSE IN ISLAMABAD"*. 5–8 words, all caps.

**Caption framework (worked example).**
> EARTHQUAKE HITS NORTHERN PAKISTAN
> A magnitude 5.8 tremor was reported near Chitral at 09:02 PKT. No casualties confirmed so far.
> Source: [outlet] · Photo: [credit]
> Follow for updates.

**CTA & hashtags.** IG: "Follow for updates" + `#BreakingNews #PakistanNews #WorldNews` · FB: "Share this update" + `#BreakingNews`.

**Canva prompt.** *"1080x1080 breaking news poster, full-bleed news photo, dark navy gradient band at bottom, red BREAKING label top-left, large white bold capital headline in 5 to 8 words, small white source line at bottom, no green, professional, no watermark."*

**Pitfalls.** Using BREAKING for routine news · wire photos without licence · headline longer than 8 words · two CTAs.

## F02 · Carousel explainer — the most copyable Instagram growth format

**Platform:** IG (works on FB) · **Evidence:** A · **Build time:** ~20 min · **Best KPI:** saves & shares

**Score profile** — Reach ★★★★☆ · Retention ★★★★★ · Clarity ★★★★★ · Engagement ★★★★☆ · Feasibility ★★★★☆

**Evidence base.** Washington Post gallery strategy (Adweek, Jul 2022): +1.2M followers in 12 months to 6M+, team of 8–9, ~10 posts/day; "images and text on a color background" with the full story in the caption so readers never leave Instagram. Al Jazeera branded social cards (Shorty Awards 2020/2021: 420M impressions 2021).

**Why it works.** Slide 1 hooks; each subsequent slide delivers exactly one fact, earning swipes (retention); the full story in the caption earns dwell time and saves; saves drive Instagram recommendation. No video, no camera, pure design.

**Structural template (1080×1080 × 5 slides):**

```
S1 navy:   [BREAKING bar] + HEADLINE + 1-line summary
S2 white:  navy number "1" + ONE FACT (1 sentence, 36–44 pt)
S3 white:  navy number "2" + ONE FACT
S4 white:  navy number "3" + ONE FACT
S5 navy:   "WHAT HAPPENS NEXT" + source list + "Follow for updates"
```

**Visual rules.** Navy/white/red only. Numbers in `#C8102E`. Optional small photo on slide 1 top-right only. One sentence per fact — if it needs two, it is two slides.

**Headline archetype:** A4 numbered list or A6 what-we-know — *"What happened in Lahore today?"* · *"The new budget: what we know"*.

**Caption framework.** First line = headline. Then the **full story** in 3–8 short sentences (this is the WaPo mechanic — the reader never needs to leave). Then source + one CTA.

**CTA & hashtags.** "Save this post" + `#BreakingNews #Explained #PakistanNews #WorldNews`.

**Canva prompt.** *"1080x1080 5-slide Instagram carousel explainer, slide 1 dark navy with red BREAKING label and large white headline, slides 2 to 4 white with large navy numbers and one fact each, slide 5 navy with what happens next, clean, no green, professional."*

**Pitfalls.** More than one fact per slide · burying the headline on slide 2 · empty caption ("link in bio" instead of the story) · 10 slides of padding (5–7 is the sweet spot).

## F03 · Branded typographic BREAKING card — the no-photo workhorse

**Platform:** FB + IG · **Evidence:** A · **Build time:** ~5 min · **Best KPI:** speed-to-publish & brand recognition

**Score profile** — Reach ★★★★☆ · Retention ★★★☆☆ · Clarity ★★★★★ · Engagement ★★★☆☆ · Feasibility ★★★★★

**Evidence base.** Al Jazeera English branded social cards — 2020: 449M+ views, 46M engagements; 2021: 355M video views, 44.5M engagements (Al Jazeera Medium reports). WaPo "text on a color background" cards (Adweek 2022).

**Why it works.** Zero photo licensing risk, 5-minute production, perfect brand recall. When news breaks and no usable photo exists, this is the format that keeps you first — and consistent card design trains the audience to recognise your posts at scroll speed.

**Structural template (1080×1080):**

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ ▬▬▬▬▬▬ red top bar ▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬ │
│ [BREAKING] box, top-left     │
│                              │
│      HEADLINE CENTRE         │  huge white caps, ≤3 lines
│      (Oswald/Montserrat)     │
│  1-line summary, light grey  │
│                              │
│ 15 NOV 2026 · 09:00 PKT      │  bottom strip
│ Source: [outlet]             │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

**Visual rules.** Solid navy `#0B1F3A`. Red-outlined white BREAKING box top-left. Gold `#C9A227` optional as a 4 px rule under the headline — never as background. No photo.

**Headline archetype:** A1 or agency-plain — *"PAKISTAN ANNOUNCES NEW BUDGET"* · *"SENAAT SESSION ADJOURNED"* (short factual, 2–6 words).

**Caption framework.** Headline first line → 2 sentences of verified fact → `Source: [outlet]` → "Follow for updates".

**CTA & hashtags.** "Follow for updates" + `#BreakingNews` (FB) / + `#BreakingNews #WorldNews #PakistanNews` (IG).

**Canva prompt.** *"1080x1080 typographic breaking news card, solid dark navy background, red top bar, white bold capital headline in centre, red BREAKING label top-left, small source line at bottom, no photo, no green, minimal professional."*

**Pitfalls.** Adding decorative imagery that ages badly · using this for stories that need evidence (photo/still) · letting the template drift — lock it.

## F04 · Timed headline bulletin — the habit builder

**Platform:** FB (IG variant below) · **Evidence:** B · **Build time:** ~12 min · **Best KPI:** consistent reach & return visits

**Score profile** — Reach ★★★★☆ · Retention ★★★☆☆ · Clarity ★★★★★ · Engagement ★★★★☆ · Feasibility ★★★★★

**Evidence base.** Geo / ARY / Dunya hourly headline bulletins (ARY "1 AM / 2 AM Headlines" convention); fixed-slot habit matches the 09:00 / 14:00 / 19:00 PKT routine. Predictability is the format.

**Why it works.** Three to five stories in one post multiplies the chance that one of them is relevant to each viewer; fixed times train the audience to come back; numbered items invite "comment 1, 2 or 3", the cheapest engagement trigger there is.

**Structural template (1080×1080):**

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ HEADLINES — 09:00 PKT, 15 NOV│  navy header band
│                              │
│ 1  Earthquake hits northern… │  red number, black Inter Bold
│ ──────────────────────────── │
│ 2  Rupee steadies against…   │
│ ──────────────────────────── │
│ 3  Talks resume in… [BREAKING│  small red tag on the ONE urgent
│                              │
│ Source: [outlet] · [outlet]   │  bottom line
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

**Visual rules.** Navy header (15% height), white body, red numbers, 1 pt grey dividers. **BREAKING tag only on the single most urgent item.** No photos.

**Headline archetype:** A4 list — each item is one line: *"Earthquake hits northern Pakistan"* (sentence case in the list; the post title is the headline).

**Caption framework.** First line *"Headlines — 09:00 PKT"*. Then the 3–5 headlines as plain text. Sources. CTA: *"Which story do you want first? Comment 1, 2 or 3."*

**CTA & hashtags.** "Comment 1, 2 or 3" + `#Headlines #PakistanNews` (IG) / `#BreakingNews` (FB).

**Canva prompt.** *"1080x1080 news headline bulletin poster, dark navy header with HEADLINES and time, 5 numbered headlines in black on white with red numbers, clean, no green, professional."*

**Pitfalls.** More than 5 items · moving the slot time · putting BREAKING on every item · mixing opinion into a bulletin.

## F05 · "What happened / What we know / What next" — the clarity format

**Platform:** FB + IG · **Evidence:** B · **Build time:** ~12 min · **Best KPI:** saves & trust

**Score profile** — Reach ★★★★☆ · Retention ★★★★☆ · Clarity ★★★★★ · Engagement ★★★★☆ · Feasibility ★★★★★

**Evidence base.** Vox / Axios explainer pattern ("what we know" journalism); pairs with the DEVELOPING label discipline (F22). The structure is the single clearest way to cover an unfolding story without overclaiming.

**Why it works.** It separates fact from open questions — the exact thing audiences can't get from a noisy feed. The three labelled blocks are scannable in two seconds and honest by construction.

**Structural template (1080×1080):**

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ [photo top 60% — free stock, │
│  darkened slightly]          │
│▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓│
│ WHAT HAPPENED — one sentence │  red label + black text
│ WHAT WE KNOW   — one sentence│
│ WHAT NEXT      — one sentence│
│ Source: [outlet]             │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

**Visual rules.** Labels: Montserrat Bold, `#C8102E`, 28 pt caps. Sentences: Inter Regular, `#111111`, 30–36 pt. Alternative: full navy card with white text when there is no photo. BREAKING bar only if genuinely urgent.

**Headline archetype:** A6 — *"What we know about the Lahore flooding"* · *"Lahore rain floods roads — what happened, what we know, what next"*.

**Caption framework.** Headline first line → the same three blocks as plain text → source → *"Save this — updates will follow."*

**CTA & hashtags.** "Save this — updates will follow" + `#BreakingNews #Explained #PakistanNews` (IG).

**Canva prompt.** *"1080x1080 breaking news poster, photo top, white bottom panel with three red labels WHAT HAPPENED, WHAT WE KNOW, WHAT NEXT, one sentence each, no green, clean professional."*

**Pitfalls.** Writing speculation into "What we know" · four blocks instead of three · sentences over 15 words.

## F06 · Short captioned Reel (15–30 seconds) — the retention format

**Platform:** IG + FB · **Evidence:** A · **Build time:** ~30 min · **Best KPI:** watch-through & follows

**Score profile** — Reach ★★★★★ · Retention ★★★★☆ · Clarity ★★★★☆ · Engagement ★★★☆☆ · Feasibility ★★★★☆

**Evidence base.** Sky News (Digiday 2016): front-load the first 3 seconds, stay under 90 sec (average watch 45 sec), subtitles always. Fox News: videos under 30 sec median 82,396 interactions. NowThis: one story = one short native video (15-second spots). AJ+ built 9B views on short captioned video.

**Why it works.** Reels are Instagram's discovery surface; sub-30-second, subtitled, front-loaded video is the only video shape that clears the retention curve without a studio. Stills + voiceover + burned-in subtitles count — no camera required.

**Structural template (1080×1920, 15–30 s, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz):**

```
0–2s   red BREAKING bar + HEADLINE on navy band (spoken AND on-screen)
2–25s  stills (slow Ken Burns zoom) or free stock clip, one cut per fact
       subtitles burned in throughout, Inter Bold 32–40 pt
last 3s end frame: navy + "Follow for updates" + Source: [outlet]
```

**Visual rules.** Navy text bands, white type, red bar. Never unlicensed news footage. Never subtitles in the bottom 15% (platform UI).

**Headline archetype:** spoken hook = the headline — *"Breaking: earthquake in northern Pakistan — here is what we know."*

**Caption framework.** Headline → 2 sentences → source → "Follow for the next update". IG hashtags: `#BreakingNews #NewsReel #WorldNews`.

**Canva prompt (cover/stills).** *"1080x1920 vertical breaking news Reel cover, dark navy background, red BREAKING label top, large white headline, no green, bold professional."*

**Pitfalls.** Slow intros ("hey guys…") · missing subtitles · over 30 s for routine news · landscape video letterboxed into 9:16.

## F07 · Score / result graphic — the most shareable single image in sport

**Platform:** FB + IG · **Evidence:** A · **Build time:** ~8 min · **Best KPI:** shares

**Score profile** — Reach ★★★★☆ · Retention ★★★☆☆ · Clarity ★★★★★ · Engagement ★★★★★ · Feasibility ★★★★★

**Evidence base.** ESPN (25.6M FB / 24M+ IG), BBC Sport, Sky Sports; ESPN + SportsCenter combined 28.0M engagements Feb 2025 (NewsWhip/MediaPost sample). Score-first structure is the fastest comprehension path in sports.

**Why it works.** The score IS the story: zero ambiguity, instant shareability among both fan bases, and a template that never changes. For Pakistan, cricket and football are the reliable beats (and elections reuse the same board — see F35).

**Structural template (1080×1080):**

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│        ▬▬ RESULT ▬▬          │  red bar, top centre
│                              │
│  PAKISTAN        INDIA       │  white caps, sides
│     180/4    vs   176/8      │  huge white numerals (120 pt)
│                              │
│ Gaddafi Stadium · 15 NOV     │  small white detail line
│ Source: [outlet]             │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

**Visual rules.** Navy `#0B1F3A` field. Team names Montserrat ExtraBold; score 120 pt. Red RESULT bar (not BREAKING). Optional small flag/crest (not photos of players).

**Headline archetype:** A2 score-first — *"PAKISTAN 180/4 BEAT INDIA 176/8"*.

**Caption framework.** Score as text → one sentence (top scorer / key moment) → source → *"Share if you watched it."*

**CTA & hashtags.** "Share if you watched it" + `#Cricket #PakistanCricket` (IG) / `#Cricket` (FB).

**Canva prompt.** *"1080x1080 cricket score graphic, dark navy background, two team names and huge white score in centre, red RESULT bar on top, clean, no green, professional sports design."*

**Pitfalls.** Player photos (rights) · stale scores posted late · BREAKING label on a completed match.

## F10 · Photo-essay carousel — "in pictures"

**Platform:** IG · **Evidence:** B · **Build time:** ~30 min · **Best KPI:** swipe-through & saves

**Score profile** — Reach ★★★★☆ · Retention ★★★★★ · Clarity ★★★★☆ · Engagement ★★★★☆ · Feasibility ★★★☆☆

**Evidence base.** NYT photo-essay tradition (NYT IG 20,835,979 — AltIndex 14 Nov 2026; 2,361 posts in 2023, MDPI); Reuters single-striking-photo discipline.

**Structural template (1080×1080 × 4–6 slides):** Slide 1: best photo full-bleed + navy headline band. Slides 2–5: one photo each, one-line caption, **credit on every slide**.

**Visual rules.** Photo-led; design elements must not introduce green (photo content itself is natural). Licence checked per photo; credit per slide; never wire photos.

**Headline archetype:** A9 in-pictures — *"Floods in Punjab — in pictures"*.
