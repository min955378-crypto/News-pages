# PART 7 — THE ANTI-PLAYBOOK: WHAT WAS EXCLUDED AND WHY

These patterns were observed across the 100 accounts and deliberately excluded from the Top 35. They are listed so nobody rebuilds them by accident.

| Excluded pattern | Where it appears | Why it is excluded |
|---|---|---|
| Live on-the-ground / war-zone reporting | BBC, CNN, Al Jazeera, TRT | Correspondents, safety kit, live feeds |
| 24/7 breaking desk, multi-post-per-hour | BBC 6–8/day, Aaj Tak, Daily Mail | Volume IS the format; a one-person account gets late and wrong. Repeatable version: 2–3 timed posts/day |
| Licensed wire photos as main visual | Reuters, AP, NYT | Copyright infringement without a licence → strikes. Use free stock or typographic cards (F03/F09) |
| Documentary / long-form video | Vice IGTV, AJ+ | Filming, editing, access |
| Celebrity-gossip screenshot machine | The Shade Room (174K posts) | Team monitoring + legal/misinformation risk (screenshot mechanics survive as F13, honestly) |
| Highlight-clip sport Reels | ESPN, Bleacher Report (70–82M engagements/mo) | Broadcast rights; never repost match clips |
| Studio/talking-head presenter Reels | Daily Wire, France 24, WION | Presenter + studio + teleprompter; possible later, not day one |
| High-volume meme curation | Pubity (42.2M, 240B views group-wide) | Copyright/credit risk; Instagram demotes unoriginal reposts |
| Market/chart graphics at speed | Bloomberg, CNBC | Needs a live data desk; one wrong number destroys trust (occasional sourced stat cards are F30) |
| Copying any big Page's photo | all | The layout is copyable; the photo is not |
